## Filament

Any filament can be used.  The prototypes of this enclosure were printed using PLA.
The body was printed using a matte finish filament to contrast against the silk finish of the bezels, buttons, and wings.  

The exact filaments used:
* Polymaker Panchroma Matte PLA Army Blue (CA04008) (Amazon ASIN B0D7ZY2STQ)
* Miailuy PLA SilkBrass (MIA030) (Amazon ASIN B0FN46RKZW)

Polymaker filament presets for your printer can be downloaded from https://presets.polymaker.com/.  
For the Miailuy filament, use the Polymaker Panchroma Silk settings.

## Printing Tips

Overall: For the best finish, use 0.08 mm or minimum layer height with a standard 0.4 mm nozzle.

| Component | Setting (may vary based on slicer) |
|---|---|
| main body front & back |	Enable support:	On (tree(auto), default style) |
| main body front & back |	Wall sequence:	Outer wall / inner |
| main body front & back | Variable layer height: Adaptive (Quality/Speed: 0, Radius: 10), Smooth x5, Keep min |
| wings right & left, buttons, all bezels | Seam position: Random |

## Optional Bezel Glass

The display bezels are designed to take watch crystals for a more finished look. Below is the specification for the crystals required and some links for examples of them.

**Specifications:**

- 35.5 mm diameter
- 2 mm edge thickness
- Double domed
- Glass (preferred)

**Sources:**

Watch crystals are considerably less expensive from China, so the example sources are all from AliExpress. There are many sources on AliExpress for these watch crystals. Vendors often sell many sizes and types. Be careful: it can be easy to order the wrong ones.

## Assembly Tips
- A helpful assembly visualization video is at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JYFkHDC2DEo
- To avoid any accidents with glue, you can instead affix the display bezels to the front body by inserting the bezels and gently melting the bezel pins into the body from the back.
- Snap in the button bezels and then turn the front body upside down to insert the button plungers. Align the buttons on the PCB with the plungers and rotate the PCB into place. The plungers are held in place by the PCB assembly.
- Be sure the joint sleeves are completely pressed into the wing bodies. Insert each joint sleeve into its wing, then turn the wing sleeve-side down on a table and press firmly.
